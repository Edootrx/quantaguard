Product

One account system, three layers.

In plain words: QuantaGuard is a wallet you can recover if you lose your key, that can hand a limited spare key to an AI agent, and that connects to a job board where solvers get paid to solve optimisation problems, the useful work QMS is built around.

01

PQ-anchored guardian recovery

Smart accounts are created through a CREATE2 factory, so the address is predictable before deployment. Each account registers a guardian in the PQGuardianRegistry. The guardian identity is anchored to a hash commitment of an ML-DSA or SLH-DSA public key.

If the owner key is lost, the guardian proposes a new owner. The change executes only after a 48 hour timelock, and the owner can cancel at any time during the window. Today guardians act with ECDSA keys; the commitment is the anchor, and the verifier is a pluggable interface that a real ML-DSA verifier fills when QMS v1 ships PQ accounts.

Live on v0.1 (commitment anchored by hash)
02

AI-agent session keys

An autonomous agent should never hold the master key. QuantaGuard issues session keys with on-chain policy: an expiry time, a cumulative spend limit, and an optional single allowed target contract.

The account contract enforces the policy on every call. Over-limit spends revert, expired keys revert, calls to non-approved targets revert, and the owner can revoke a key at any time. Try the policy simulator in the Live Demos section below.

Live on v0.1
03

QUBO job board

QMS validators and solvers exist to solve QUBO optimisation problems. The job board is the demand side: a client locks a QMS reward, posts a problem hash, solvers submit a solution hash with an objective score, and the lowest score is paid on settlement.

Jobs can be cancelled with a full refund before any submission arrives, and settlement is reentrancy guarded. On v0.1 scores are claimed, not verified on chain; the verifier hook is isolated in the design and lands with the v2 useful-work marketplace integration.

Prototype live on v0.1

How it sustains itself

A small fee (2%) on settled QUBO jobs, plus a Pro tier for advanced agent policies and scheduled optimisation. Solvers and miners earn the locked job reward; QuantaGuard earns only when useful work actually settles. No token planned. Revenue follows real Proof-of-Useful-Work demand.
